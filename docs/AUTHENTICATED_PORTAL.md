# Authenticated portal implementation
The POST /customers/{customer_id}/portal-sessions integration issues a portal bearer link only after a current passkey session and one-time email proof sent to the canonical Paddle customer email. It never trusts an email entered by a browser. Proof expires in five minutes, is bound to owner, customer, subscription, email and session, and is consumed atomically. A customer-specific mail rate cap protects recipients. The portal response must match the customer and subscription and trusted HTTPS Paddle origin.

Activation requires an approved transactional sender and the BILLING_EMAIL Worker send binding, COURSEACCESS_BILLING_FROM_EMAIL, and a new randomly generated secret COURSEACCESS_PORTAL_PROOF_SECRET. No paid service or sender domain is provisioned automatically; the existing email-authenticated Paddle portal remains available in production. Cloudflare Email Sending to arbitrary recipients needs Workers Paid and an onboarded sender domain. The included tests use a local SQLite database, mocked provider and email delivery; they are not real email or payment tests.
